==== 19_getting_started: CSV -> SummarizedExperiment bridge (2026-08-05) ====
Round scope: document the exact workflow a wet-lab/clinical user follows to
feed their own two CSV files (counts + clinical) into rnaSentry. No R code,
tests, or gate numbers changed -> Gate 1 (147/457/0/0/32), as-cran, BiocCheck,
and the sweep allow-list are all unchanged (docs-only round).

[WHY] rnaSentry accepts a SummarizedExperiment; users start with counts.csv
      + clinical.csv. The bridge is pure user-side boilerplate (read, intersect
      sample IDs, build SE) and was previously undocumented end-to-end.
[NEW] vignettes/getting-started.Rmd (VignetteIndexEntry "Getting started: from
      two CSVs to a SummarizedExperiment"): writes seeded synthetic raw counts
      + clinical (time/event/batch/stage) to a temp dir, reads them back with
      read.csv exactly like a user, demonstrates the intersect() sample-ID
      pitfall with a planted S31/S32 mismatch (before/after dims), shows
      load_counts() + the flag ledger catching a planted non-integer count and
      an all-zero gene, runs design_audit(), then a fast run_rnaSentry()
      (render_report = FALSE). Fully offline; prints no pinned decimals.

      load_counts(), design_audit(), commented run_rnaSentry) + notes on

      the time/event requirement; Documentation section lists the vignette.
